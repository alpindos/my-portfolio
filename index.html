Hi, I'm Alpin Yalçı

Aeronautical Engineer | Technical Leader | Consultant

I'm an aeronautical engineer with over 28 years of experience in aerospace, defense, and automotive engineering. My career has taken me from working with military helicopters to developing complex engineering systems, leading technical teams, and supporting international projects.
Along the way, I've had the opportunity to work in different industries, take on new responsibilities, and learn from people with a wide range of backgrounds. I enjoy learning new things, exploring different perspectives, and finding practical solutions to challenging problems.
This website is a place to share a little more about my professional journey and personal interests. Over time, it may also become a space for sharing ideas and reflections on subjects that interest me.

Systems Engineer, R&D

Togg (Turkiye's Automobile Joint Venture Group), Ankara-Turkiye

September 2022 - June 2025

After many years in the defense industry, I had the opportunity to apply my engineering experience in another high-tech industry and joined Togg, an electric vehicle manufacturer in Türkiye. I initially worked on Drive-by-Wire systems, focusing on systems engineering, requirements, and system architecture, as well as contributing to prototype development and testing. Later, I moved into Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS), taking on a Feature Owner role and coordinating engineering activities related to feature development.
Working in the automotive industry introduced me to different engineering practices, helped me become more familiar with model-based development, and gave me a new perspective on software-intensive systems.

Engineering & Technical Leadership

Roketsan Missile Industries Inc., Ankara-Turkiye

February 2005 - August 2022

I spent more than 17 years at Roketsan, working on guided missile and launcher systems in a variety of engineering and leadership roles. I started in system design and platform integration, gradually taking on broader responsibilities in engineering management, system development, and technical decision-making.
One of the most significant parts of my career was serving as the technical authority for major missile development programs. I worked with multidisciplinary engineering teams, coordinating activities from requirements and conceptual design through system integration, testing, and qualification. I also had the opportunity to lead a dedicated platform integration unit and contribute to the integration of missile systems onto different military platforms.
In my later years at Roketsan, I served as Chief Engineer for anti-tank missile systems, focusing on product lifecycle management, technical support, and new integration activities. Throughout this period, working with customers, partners, and engineering teams from different backgrounds became an important part of my professional experience.

Engineer

Army Aviation Depot, Ankara-Turkiye

December 1998 - August 2004

My engineering career began with military helicopters at the 5th Main Maintenance Factory Directorate of the Turkish Army. I worked primarily on helicopter structural repairs and maintenance engineering, gaining hands-on experience in depot-level maintenance and overhaul.
During those years, I developed a broader understanding of helicopters beyond my immediate responsibilities, studying flight principles, mechanics, and helicopter systems through independent learning, specialized training, and collaboration with engineers and technicians from different disciplines.
